# Coverage-Testing Methodology

[Home](../README.md) | [Community Coverage Guide](../docs/coverage.md) |
[Coverage Data](README.md)

This document is an initial framework for a repeatable Harnett Mesh field-test
methodology. The purpose is to produce useful, comparable, privacy-preserving
evidence for the public coverage map.

## Test equipment

Record approved radio devices, phones, antennas, batteries, and other
equipment. Device list and approval criteria: **TBD.**

## Device configuration

Record firmware, app version, power-related settings, and other relevant
configuration without publishing private keys or sensitive configuration.
Standard test configuration: **TBD.**

## Test procedure

Define preparation, test locations, timing, message sequence, retries, and
conditions to record. Repeatable procedure: **TBD.**

## GPS/location collection

Define how location is collected, accuracy is recorded, and coordinates are
generalized or withheld before public release. Collection and privacy process:
**TBD.**

## Signal measurements

Identify which device-reported or observational measurements should be captured,
with units and limitations. Measurements: **TBD.**

## Successful and failed message tests

Define what counts as a sent, received, acknowledged, delayed, or failed
message, and how retries are recorded. Success criteria: **TBD.**

## Data format

Define the public and private data fields, file format, timestamp convention,
test identifier, and versioning approach. Data format: **TBD.**

## Quality classification

Define how observations are assigned to Strong, Good, Marginal, or Untested and
how estimated results differ from field-verified results. Thresholds and
classification rules: **TBD.**

## Submission process

Define who may submit results, the submission template, required evidence,
review queue, and correction process. Submission process: **TBD.**

## Validation

Define review, duplicate handling, repeat testing, confidence labels, and
publication approval. Validation process: **TBD.**

## Privacy considerations

Do not publish exact infrastructure locations, private property details,
personal information, private messages, credentials, keys, or other sensitive
operational information. Define retention, access, consent, and location
generalization rules before collecting public submissions. Detailed privacy
policy: **TBD.**

For the community-facing explanation of coverage and the future public map, see
the [Community Coverage Guide](../docs/coverage.md). This technical methodology
does not publish exact repeater locations or other sensitive infrastructure
information.
