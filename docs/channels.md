Harnett Mesh intends to establish standardized community channels so users can
find the right place for common conversations without inventing incompatible
local conventions.

Final channel names, identifiers, keys, configuration, moderation, and
publication rules are **TBD**. Do not treat the placeholders below as active
channels.

## Planned channel types

### County-wide community channel

A broadly available channel for county-wide community communication. Name,
purpose, access, and configuration: **TBD.**

### Geographic or community channels

Channels for appropriate geographic areas or communities. Naming scheme,
boundaries, access, and configuration: **TBD.**

### Special-purpose channels

Channels for approved interests, testing, education, or other defined purposes.
Categories, eligibility, and configuration: **TBD.**

## Channel etiquette

Users should be respectful, concise, and mindful of limited radio capacity.
Avoid sharing sensitive personal or operational information. Final etiquette,
moderation expectations, and enforcement process: **TBD.**

## Public and private channels

The distinction between public and private channels, including how keys are
managed and what may be documented publicly, is **TBD**. Private channel keys
must never be committed to this public repository.

If channel participation does not work as expected, start with
[Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md). For the planned setup path, return to
[Getting Started](getting-started.md).
