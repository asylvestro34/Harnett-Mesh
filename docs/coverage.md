Harnett Mesh plans to publish a public map that helps users understand where
communication may be possible. The map will be a planning aid, not a guarantee
of service.

## Planned public categories

- **Strong:** Conditions where testing or modelling indicates a relatively
  reliable connection.
- **Good:** Conditions where communication is generally expected but may vary.
- **Marginal:** Conditions where communication may be intermittent or limited.
- **Untested:** No sufficient model or field evidence is currently available.

The definitions and display rules for these categories are **TBD** and should
be approved after field testing.

## Estimated and field-verified coverage

**Estimated/modelled coverage** is derived from planning information such as
terrain, antenna assumptions, propagation models, or other analysis. It helps
identify areas for investigation but is not proof of service.

**Field-verified coverage** is based on documented tests performed under stated
conditions. Results should include enough context to understand the test without
revealing sensitive infrastructure information.

The map should make these two evidence types visibly distinct.

## What affects coverage

Coverage depends on terrain, antenna characteristics, device hardware, device
placement, elevation, power, environmental conditions, interference, network
load, and other radio-frequency factors. A result in one location or time does
not guarantee the same result elsewhere or later.

## Privacy and infrastructure protection

The public map must not display exact repeater locations, exact infrastructure
coordinates, private sites, or information that makes protected infrastructure
easy to identify. Generalized areas and appropriately aggregated test results
may be published after review.

See [the coverage-testing methodology](../coverage/methodology.md) and
[infrastructure documentation guidance](../infrastructure/README.md).

If you are setting up a device, use the
[Getting Started](getting-started.md) guide. If a result does not match
expectations, see [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).
