## What is Harnett Mesh?

It is a community communications initiative using MeshCore and LoRa technology
to support off-grid text communication in Harnett County. It is in planning and
early development. See the [Overview](overview.md) for the project vision.

## What is MeshCore?

MeshCore is the software and networking technology selected for this project.
The exact supported software, devices, and versions for Harnett Mesh are **TBD**.

## What is LoRa?

LoRa is a low-power radio technology designed for long-range, low-data-rate
communication. Actual range and reliability vary with equipment and conditions.

## Do I need an amateur radio license?

This depends on the device, radio configuration, frequency, and applicable
regulations. Harnett Mesh will publish reviewed guidance before onboarding users.
**Regulatory guidance: TBD; confirm before presenting as a project rule.**

## Do I need Internet access?

The intended radio communication path does not depend on Internet access.
Internet may still be useful for downloading software, documentation, or updates.

## Do I need cellular service?

Cellular service is not intended to be required for the off-grid radio path.
Phone and device requirements will depend on the approved hardware and app.

## How far can I communicate?

There is no single guaranteed distance. Terrain, antennas, device hardware,
elevation, power, interference, and environmental conditions all matter. Public
coverage information will be based on modelling and field testing as available.
See [Coverage](coverage.md) for how that information is intended to be
presented.

## Is Harnett Mesh monitored by emergency services?

Do not assume that it is monitored by emergency services. Monitoring,
coordination, and escalation arrangements are **TBD**.

## Can I use Harnett Mesh to contact 911?

No. Harnett Mesh is not a 911 service and must not be relied on for emergency
dispatch. Use established emergency services and procedures.

## Is communication private?

Do not assume that messages are private, confidential, or guaranteed to be
secure. The project’s privacy and channel guidance, including any technical
limitations, is **TBD**.

## Who operates the infrastructure?

Members of Harnett County AuxComm are developing and coordinating the initiative.
Specific ownership, maintenance, and operating responsibilities are **TBD**.
Harnett Mesh is not presented as an official Harnett County government service.

## How can I participate?

Community members will be able to follow the onboarding guide, use approved
devices and channels, provide field-test feedback, and contribute documentation
or ideas. Start with [Getting Started](getting-started.md) when the onboarding
details are available. The participation process and supported devices are
**TBD**.
