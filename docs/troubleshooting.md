This is an initial framework. Exact steps will be added after supported devices,
app versions, and Harnett Mesh standards are established. If a problem involves
private configuration or infrastructure, do not post sensitive details publicly.

## My device will not connect to my phone

Check that the device and phone are supported, powered, nearby, and using the
approved connection method. Device-specific steps and permissions: **TBD.**

## I cannot see other nodes

Confirm that the device is configured for Harnett Mesh and that the test
location is within a known or possible coverage area. Node visibility behavior
and diagnostic steps: **TBD.**

## My messages are not being delivered

Check the selected channel, destination, device power, and apparent coverage.
Message-status meanings, retry guidance, and test procedure: **TBD.**

## I can receive but cannot send

Record the device, app version, channel, location category, and observed
behavior without sharing private keys. Likely causes and approved diagnostic
steps: **TBD.**

## I appear to be outside coverage

Review the public map and its evidence type, then compare results from a safe
nearby location if appropriate. Coverage boundaries are not guarantees.
Recommended test and reporting process: **TBD.**

## How do I determine whether the network is working?

Use an approved status or test channel and follow the documented connectivity
check. Test destination, status source, and escalation process: **TBD.**

## Reporting a problem

Before opening an issue, note the time, general area (not an exact private
location), device and app versions, relevant channel type, and what happened.
Do not include message contents, keys, credentials, exact infrastructure
locations, or sensitive operational information. Public support process: **TBD.**

For general coverage concepts, see [Coverage](coverage.md). For common
beginner questions, see the [FAQ](faq.md).
